How I test

  1. 01
    Scope

    Agree what is in bounds, the rules and the stop conditions.

  2. 02
    Recon

    Map the surface: ports, services and hidden content.

  3. 03
    Exploit

    Prove the weakness with the smallest safe step.

  4. 04
    Escalate

    Show how far it goes: privileges, pivoting, data reach.

  5. 05
    Report

    Clear findings, evidence, a risk rating and the fix.

  6. 06
    Retest

    Check the fix holds and close the finding.

The Arch way, and why it suits the job

Simplicity

Nothing hidden. You see every part of the system, the same way you read a target.

Control

You build it from nothing, so you know what runs and why. Good habit for enumeration.

Minimal

No bloat, only what you choose to install. Fewer surprises, smaller attack surface.

Read the wiki

Break it, read the docs, fix it, repeat. It is the same loop as every lab I write up.

To every Arch user out there: we know, we mention it, and we would not have it any other way. Respect.
